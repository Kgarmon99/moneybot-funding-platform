The Opportunity

A generation is learning about money from TikTok.

75% of American teens lack basic financial literacy. Only 23 states require personal finance in high schools. Parents want to teach their kids but don't know how, and kids are left to costly trial-and-error.

The Solution

An AI tutor that adapts to every child.

MoneyBot combines conversational AI, gamified lessons, and real-world practice to teach kids how to earn, save, spend, and invest. Each interaction is personalized to age, knowledge level, and interests.

AI Tutor

Kids ask anything from "What's a stock?" to "How do I start a business?" and get answers tailored to their level.

Gamified Learning

Quests, streaks, and rewards turn financial habits into a game kids actually want to play.

Parent Dashboard

Parents track progress, set goals, and get prompts for real-world money conversations.
