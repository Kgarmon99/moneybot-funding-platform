Executive Summary

The problem: 75% of American teens lack basic financial literacy. Only 23 states require personal finance in high schools. Parents want to teach their kids but don’t know how, and kids are left learning from TikTok or costly trial-and-error.

Our solution: MoneyBot meets kids on their phones with an AI tutor that adapts to each child’s age, knowledge level, and interests. Kids go from “What’s a stock?” to explaining compound interest to their parents.

Traction: Launched January 2026. In 4 months we reached 10 schools, 120+ signed-up students, and 200+ weekly active users — all organically, with zero marketing spend. Local news stations in Louisville and Lexington have reached out to cover the story.

Market Opportunity

50 million kids aged 8–16 in the US. Capturing just 1% at $80/year ARPU equals a $40M ARR opportunity domestically.

The B2B school market alone is an $8B+ annual spend on curriculum, tools, and professional development. Schools are desperate for modern financial literacy solutions that actually engage students.

Indirect competitors (Khan Academy, YouTube, books) are passive and one-size-fits-all. Direct competitors (Greenlight, GoHenry, FamZoo) focus on spending controls, not education. MoneyBot owns the learning layer.
