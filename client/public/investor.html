FAQ

What investors ask us.

What makes MoneyBot different from Khan Academy or Greenlight?

We're AI-native and conversation-first. Kids learn by asking questions, not watching videos. We also connect parents to the learning loop in a way fintech apps don't.

How do you acquire customers?

Free school pilots create teacher champions. Students use MoneyBot in class, then bring it home. Parents convert to paid subscriptions.

What is the path to revenue?

B2C subscriptions launch first. School district licenses and employer-sponsored family benefits follow once we have measurable usage and outcomes.

How do you keep kids safe?

COPPA-compliant from day one. No ads, no data selling, age-gated conversations, and parent-managed accounts.
